# PSID trial specification (before regression)

Keep the existing NLS article unchanged. Use biological mother–child links and
individual real labor earnings from PSID-SHELF V2.1. Income age is survey year
minus one minus birth year. Earnings cover reference persons/spouses, not every
adult household member. Farming income is not included after 1993, according to
the official construction code; this comparability limitation must be reported.

Exclude missing, negative, and standardized nominal topcodes (9,999,999).
Include valid zero earnings in averages. Require at least three observed years
at ages 30–39 for both generations. Select the single observation closest to 35
(earlier year breaks ties), then require both selected incomes and averages to
be positive. Never choose a different observation because its income is positive.

Primary comparison: single-year versus multiyear-average log-log OLS slopes in
exactly the same sample. Cluster uncertainty by mother; compare paired slopes.

Age comparison: hold mother's ages-30–39 average fixed and use exactly the same
children at all three ages. Select observations nearest 22, 35, 45 within 20–24,
30–39, 40–49 respectively, then require all three to be positive. Compare slopes
with mother-clustered paired uncertainty. No sample tuning based on estimates.

Estimates are unweighted, descriptive, selected-sample associations, not national
estimates or causal effects. Age sensitivity is not proof of bias relative to an
unobserved true lifetime IGE. No significance requirement is imposed.
